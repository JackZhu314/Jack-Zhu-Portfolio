Lulu

Oil and Pastel on Canvas

Size

55.1 x 39.3 in

Year

2023

Description

When we talk about connections, discussions typically begin from a first-person viewpoint. However, it struck me that the act of connecting is a two-way street, necessitating an understanding of others' perspectives. This realization led me to closely observe my dog's behaviors and interactions within our shared living space. Through this piece, I aimed to portray her potential interpretations of the world around her. I set the color into a turquoise pallet according to the scientific research of the optic color that dogs perceive. The picture captures our daily interactions, framed by the objects she selects as mediums to engage with us. In shifting the perspective to her point of view, I not only celebrated the unique bond we share but also emphasized the importance of appreciating connections from multiple angles, ultimately fostering a deeper understanding and empathy for all our relationships.
