Carta Social System

A Figma and Claude powered library. Internship capstone.

Social Template Library is a capstone project I built as a Brand Design Intern at Carta to fix a real bottleneck: static and carousel posts made up 25–40% of Carta's social content, each requiring four differently sized files with no shared system to keep them on-brand. I designed a Figma library of 304 templates across 76 components and 9 categories, ready to export in four formats, with an intuitive layer system so anyone can edit safely without breaking the design.

To push the workflow even further, I used Figma's MCP to build a custom Claude Skill that turns post creation into a simple, guided conversation. Anyone at the company, no design background required, can describe what they're posting and have Claude interview them, surface matching templates, and generate a finished, on-brand post on their own. Every post it creates lands in a review page where the brand team can quickly confirm it's on-brand. Together, these cut post production time from 2–4 days to about an hour.

Static posts and carousels make up 25–40% of B2B LinkedIn  content — at Carta, they're the most requested design format by volume.

Static posts and carousels alone generate ~8 design requests per month in 2026. Constantly recurring.

One post requires four files: LinkedIn Portrait (1080×1350), Square (1080×1080), Landscape (1920×1080), and X/Thread (1200×675).

Without a shared foundation, visual decisions vary  post to post  — and inconsistency builds faster than anyone notices
