“GRANTED”

A campaign about immigrant experiences.

Campaign Design

Game Design

Year

2026

Credits

Jack Zhu

Yewon Choi

Kunha Baek

Description

“GRANTED” is an interactive campaign that explores how identity is processed and recognized within institutional structures. The experience spans a digital interface and a physical board game, simulating a journey from entry to life in a new country. In the web component, users respond to timed prompts modeled after an immigration checkpoint. Each response generates a stamp, forming a personalized passport across six conditions: Entry, Language, Social, System, Memory, and Internal State. The system does not define identity, it interprets it. The board game extends this process beyond arrival. Players navigate events and collect stamps tied to life milestones such as housing, work, and identification. Across both formats, stamps function as a shared language, produced digitally and reinforced through print, linking actions, decisions, and progression into a single recorded experience.
