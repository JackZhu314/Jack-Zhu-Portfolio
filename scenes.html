Scenes

Pen on Paper

Size

18.1 x 21.6 in

Year

2023

Description

The emotional connection extends beyond human interaction; it transcends to the very places we encounter. Entering a specific site becomes a transformative experience as one's surroundings trigger a multitude of emotions, deeply influenced by individual perception. I chose to depict three different scenarios, in this case intentionally emphasizing the ones that would invoke stress and anxiety, to capture this intricate connection. Visually, I adopted cinematographic compositions and erased the trace of figures, so that the viewers can step into these immersive settings and forge a direct connection with them, enabling them to experience the emotions I sought to convey and to reflect on the relationship between our inner world and the external environment.
